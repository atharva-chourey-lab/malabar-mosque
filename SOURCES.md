## 2. The fabric

| Claim in paper | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Location and form | Methala, Kodungallur, historic core of Muziris/Mahodayapuram; Kerala temple-type mosque: laterite-and-timber, sloping tiled roofs, prayer hall in local idiom, pond, wooden minbar, brass hanging lamp ("Cheraman lamp") of temple type; nothing Arabian or 7th-century in form | MOS §2 |
| Surviving traditional structure | Dated to the 14th–15th century (type and fabric) — Shokoohy, *Muslim Architecture of South India* (2003), pp. 139–142 | MOS §2 |
| Granite foundation | Dated stylistically to the 11th–12th century (survey literature); oldest datable fabric | MOS §2; MOS §6 verdict |
| Phasing | Renovation assigned to the 11th century; major rebuild conventionally dated 1568; renovations 1974 and 2001; concrete extension with minarets and dome 1984; restoration completed 2022 under the Muziris Heritage Project, returning the exterior to the traditional Kerala form | MOS §2 |
| Foundation inscription | None with a date — no Hijri or Kollam-era date at any foundation moment; "inscription dates it to 1124" claim conflates Kodungallur with the Madayi slab of AH 518 / 1124 CE | MOS §2; MOS §3.3 |
| Brass lamp inscription | Old Malayalam (Vattezhuthu) script; no published reading dates it to the early period; Vattezhuthu in use for a millennium, so script alone cannot carry a 7th-century date | MOS §2 |
| Artefacts | Wooden minbar, lamp, block of white marble said to have been brought from Mecca: claimed antiquities, none dated by inscription or published technical study | MOS §2 |
| Fabric verdict | Oldest datable element 11th–12th c. (granite foundation); oldest surviving superstructure type 14th–15th c.; no element or inscription within four centuries of 629 | MOS §2, "Fabric verdict" |

## 7. Gaps flagged in the paper

1. **Epigraphy graded probable, not firm.** The Panthalayani tombstone and the Koyilandy inscription circulate through survey literature rather than a modern epigraphic edition available to the source build; the paper carries them as probable throughout, and §9 of the paper makes the 782/783 grade explicitly hostage to a critical edition of the tombstone.
2. **The *Qiṣṣat* is undated.** No dated colophon is available in the source; the paper therefore grades it as evidence for the legend's developed form only, and lists a dated colophon among the evidence that would change the grades.
3. **No scientific dating of the building.** No published radiocarbon or OSL dating of the Kodungallur mosque's timbers or foundation exists in the source; the fabric grades rest on stylistic and survey dating (Shokoohy; survey literature) and are stated as such.
4. **Data/code availability beyond the source file** is not stated in the source file; the paper claims none beyond the local build file named in its Data & Code Availability note.
